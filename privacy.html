Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026 · Last updated: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Jack Dyke, doing business as Touchstone (“we,” “us”), collects and uses information through this website and our text-message program.

Information we collect

Information you give us through the contact form or by phone or email: your name, email address, mobile phone number, your message, and your text-message consent choices.

How we use it

To respond to your inquiry, provide real estate services to you as our client, schedule showings and appointments, and — only if you opted in — send text messages as described in our SMS Terms.

No sharing or selling of mobile information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All text-message opt-in data and consent information is excluded from any sharing and will never be sold, rented, or shared with any third party. We do not buy phone numbers or lead lists. Information may be shared only with service providers that help us operate our text messaging (such as our carrier partner) solely to deliver messages to you, or when required by law.

Data security & retention

We use reasonable safeguards and keep information only as long as needed to serve you or as required by law.

Your choices

Reply STOP to any text to opt out. To access or delete your information, email jdyke@tcpre.com or call (415) 518-7523.
